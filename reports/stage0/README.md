## Coordinate support and relocation

Use at least 20 valid paired departure coordinates in a quarter before its median can enter a distance or relocation check. The raw medians of 24 rejected station-quarters remain in `quarterly_coordinates.csv` with `coordinate_usable=False`. Low-support coordinates do not enter identity matching or representative centers.

Distances use EPSG:32618 in metres. A displacement must exceed 50 metres across supported quarters to trigger a relocation flag. **Flagged within-ID stations: 0.** IDs with fewer than two supported quarters remain explicitly unaudited for relocation. Missing coordinates never become zero distance.

Operation overlap is the intersection of the two observed first/last-date intervals in calendar days. More than 14 days takes priority over handoff merging. Exactly 14 overlap days or an absolute handoff gap of exactly 14 days remains eligible for renumbering. A positive gap above 14 days is an interrupted reopening. Missing coordinates, relocation, concurrency, and unresolved timelines exclude both identities from station pools. A merge preserves the earliest ID and opening date. Exclusions propagate through approved merge chains; conflicting predecessor identities are excluded without fusing distinct stations.

## Continuous station identities and conservation

`station_id_map.csv` maps all 385 raw nonvirtual IDs to 365 canonical station records. `canonical_station_registry.csv` preserves membership, earliest opening, first/last activity, and explicit identity exclusion reasons. Identity-cleared pre-2022 status does not yet assert that baseline volume or inactivity criteria have passed.

`load_trips(..., station_id_map=...)` remaps both endpoints before later same-physical-station cleaning. Excluded endpoints do not erase valid departures from another station. The ignored `data/interim/identity_weekly_departures.csv` combines aliases and includes zero weeks across interruptions. It is an identity activity audit, before short/round-trip panel cleaning; apply the inactivity rule only to the continuous identity. The canonical weekly grid has 56,940 rows and preserves 3,210,064 departures within the complete-week study window, enforced by a failing assertion.

## Reproduction

```powershell
python -m pip install -e '.[dev]'
python scripts/run_stage0.py
```

Use `--offline` for already downloaded inputs; checksums are verified before auditing. URLs and SHA256 hashes for archives and extracted CSVs remain pinned in `data/manifest.json`. Source discovery uses Indego's official public media catalog because its data-page HTML lists stale 2017 links. Raw and intermediate inputs stay outside Git. No radius counting or treatment-effect fitting is performed by this command.
